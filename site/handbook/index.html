    01

    What WorkDesk is

    WorkDesk is GALIL's internal desk for reported time, planned weeks, tasks, expenses, budgets and approvals. One person's day goes in at the top; the company's utilisation, budget burn and payroll record come out at the bottom.

    Open it in a browser — it is a single page, and V7 runs standalone with no server behind it. Everything you enter is stored in your own browser under the key galil-workdesk-v1, and your signed-in session under galil-workdesk-session.

    Before you rely on it

    In the standalone build, clearing your browser data deletes the company's records. Whoever administers WorkDesk should download a backup regularly — see chapter 15. Microsoft sign-in and the shared database are simulated locally in V7; the rules, routing and messages are the real ones.

    The two hour colours

    Every hour you report is one of two kinds, and almost every rule in WorkDesk follows from which one you pick.

    Red · Company
    Internal time. Never billed to a client, never drawn from a project budget, and never blocked — Red hours register straight away whatever else is going on.
    Yellow · Client
    Billable time. Drawn from your allowance on the assignment and from the project or work-order budget. Yellow is the hour type the entitlement engine checks.

    Sick and Vacation are day types rather than hour types. They record one day each and always register directly.

    The words WorkDesk uses

    TermWhat it means here
    ClientThe organisation the work is for. GALIL (internal) is a client too — it is where Red hours sit.
    ProjectA body of work for one client. Carries a status, an owner and optionally a budget.
    Work orderA contracted slice of a project, coded WO-[client initials]-[6 digits]. A project marked WO tracked requires one on every entry.
    TaskA unit of work inside a project, with a status, a priority, assignees and optionally an hour allowance.
    AssignmentThe link between a person and a project or work order. It carries that person's allowance in hours. No assignment means Yellow time goes to approval.
    AllowanceHours one person may report on one assignment. Blank means unlimited within the project budget; 0 means assigned but not yet funded.
    BudgetHours (or money, pages, words, custom units) agreed for a project or work order, across everyone.
    Entitlement engineThe checks that decide, the moment you save, whether an entry registers or goes to approval. See chapter 5.

    Dates, times and numbers

    Dates display Israeli-style, 11/09/2026, and long dates as Friday, 11/09/2026. Times are 24-hour. Hours are derived from start and end and rounded to the nearest half hour — you never type a duration. Money shows with its currency symbol; ₪, $ and € are available.

    02

    Signing in and signing up

    Only @galiltc.co.il addresses may sign in. External contractors do not sign themselves up — an administrator provisions them.

    Sign in with Microsoft

    1. Click Sign in with Microsoft.
    2. Approve the request in Microsoft Authenticator — this is where the company MFA policy is enforced.
    3. Choose your account from the list.

    In the standalone build the account chooser lists the seeded demo accounts so you can try any role — see the reference chapter.

    Sign in with email and password

    1. Type your company email and password, then press Enter or click Sign in.
    2. If the address does not end in @galiltc.co.il, WorkDesk refuses it before checking the password.
    Lockout

    Five consecutive failures lock the account and count down the minutes remaining before you can try again.

    Forgotten password

    Click Forgot password, enter your company address, and a single-use reset link valid for 30 minutes is sent (simulated in the standalone build).

    Creating an account — the four-step wizard

    Click Create an account on the sign-in card. The wizard will not let you past a step with an error on it.

    Step 1 · Identity

    Full name in English (Hebrew optional), company email, mobile number for MFA enrolment, and either a password or a linked Microsoft account.

    A password must be at least 12 characters and contain an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a digit.

    Step 2 · Role

    Employee is the default. You may self-select Employee, Senior Employee or Team Lead. Every role above Team Lead is marked needs approval — requesting one creates an extra administrator task on top of the normal activation request.

    Step 3 · Profile

    Job title and department are required. Team, direct manager, start date, contracted weekly and daily hours, employment type and a profile photo are optional but worth filling in — contracted hours drive the capacity warning in the Week Planner.

    Step 4 · Confirm

    Check the summary, accept the internal usage policy, and click Submit for activation.

    Waiting for activation

    The account exists immediately but is held. Until an Operations Manager or the CEO activates it, signing in shows the holding screen and nothing else — see chapter 14.

    Sessions and signing out

    A session lasts 12 hours of inactivity. Concurrent sessions on several devices are permitted and can be revoked. Open your profile from the top-right corner to see your sessions and to sign out.

    Every sign-in and sign-out is written to the audit log with actor, timestamp and device.

    03

    Roles and permissions

    Nine roles. What you see, what you may change, and whose records you may touch all follow from yours — including which tabs appear at all.

    The nine roles

    CodeRoleWhat it carries
    EMPEmployeeReports own time, plans own week, reports own expenses, works assigned tasks, writes to the Library.
    SENSenior EmployeeAs Employee, plus may create draft tasks within assigned projects and see their full task board.
    TLTeam LeadAs Senior Employee, plus approves time and expenses for their team and manages task assignment.
    PMProject ManagerAs Team Lead, plus owns projects, work orders, budgets and assignments.
    OPSOperations ManagerAs Project Manager across all clients. Manages master lists, employees, roles and settings.
    FINFinanceRead-only across budgets, expenses, work orders and exports. Cannot approve time.
    CEOCEO / OwnerFull access, including role assignment, deletion, audit log and company settings.
    EXTExternal ContractorReports time only against explicitly assigned work orders. No budgets, Library or Dashboard.
    VIEWViewerRead-only dashboard access for a named stakeholder. No reporting rights.

    Scope letters

    A permission is not simply on or off — it carries a scope that says whose records it reaches.

    Y permitted, company-wide O own records only T own team only P own projects only – denied

    T · own team means: people sharing your team, people who report to you, and everyone in a team you lead. P · own projects means: projects you own, plus projects you are assigned to, and everyone who has reported time or holds an assignment on them.

    The full permission matrix

    This is the same matrix the application shows under ⚙ → Roles & permissions.

    Which tabs each role sees

    Tabs are hidden rather than disabled, so two people can be looking at very different applications.

    RoleTabs shown
    EMP SENTime Log · Week Planner · Tasks · Expenses · Dashboard · Library
    TLTime Log · Week Planner · Tasks · Expenses · Approvals · Dashboard · Library — no Budgets
    PM OPS CEOTime Log · Week Planner · Tasks · Expenses · Budgets · Approvals · Dashboard · Library
    FINTime Log (read-only) · Tasks · Budgets · Dashboard · Library
    EXTTime Log · Week Planner · Tasks · Expenses
    VIEWTime Log (read-only) · Dashboard

    Budget visibility starts at Project Manager — a Team Lead approves their team's time without seeing what the project is worth.

    How a role is set or changed

    • At sign-up you request a role. Anything above Team Lead raises a Role change request as well as the activation request.
    • An Operations Manager or the CEO confirms the role at activation — they may change it before activating.
    • Afterwards, the role is changed in ⚙ → People using the dropdown in the Role column. Every change is written to the audit log as Role change EMP → SEN for ….
    • Only OPS and CEO may manage employees and roles. Nobody else, at any level, can promote anyone.

    05

    Time Log

    Where the day is reported. A form band at the top, a month of entries below it, and an engine in between that decides whether each entry registers or goes to an approver.

    Reporting time

    1. Set the Date, or click Today. Future dates are refused — plan those in the Week Planner instead.
    2. Choose the Type: Work, Sick or Vacation. Sick and Vacation record one day and hide the time fields.
    3. Set Start and End from the hour and minute dropdowns. Hours fills itself, rounded to the nearest half hour.
    4. Pick the Hour type — Red · Company or Yellow · Client.
    5. For Yellow, choose Client, Project, and Work order if the project is work-order tracked. Optionally pick a Task and set the Project status.
    6. Write a Description — it is required. Notes are optional.
    7. Click Add entry.
    Type it

    Each of Client, Project and Work order has a Type it toggle for something not yet on the master list. Typing a name is allowed, but the entry then goes to approval as an Unlisted item, and on approval the name becomes a real master-list record.

    What the form refuses to save

    • End time not later than start time.
    • An empty description.
    • Yellow time with no client, or no project. Switch to Red if it is internal.
    • No work order on a project that is tracked by work order.
    • An overlap with another entry you already reported that day — the message names the clashing times.
    • An identical entry (same date, same start and end, same description).
    • A future date.
    • A date inside a month that has been closed for payroll.

    The entitlement engine

    The moment you save, WorkDesk runs these checks in order. The first one that fails sets the entry to Pending, records the reason, and raises an approval request.

    #CheckOutcome
    1Sick or VacationRegisters directly.
    2Red · CompanyRegisters directly. Red is never gated.
    3Unlisted client, project or work orderPending — Unlisted item.
    4Assigned to that project or work order?Pending — Not assigned.
    5Task selected but not assigned to you, or already Done / CancelledPending — Task not assigned or Task closed.
    6Your allowance on that assignmentPending — Allowance exceeded.
    7Project or work-order budgetPending — Budget exceeded. Escalates to the project owner rather than your team lead.
    8Daily hard limit (default 12 h)Pending — Daily limit exceeded.
    9Daily soft limit (default 9 h)Registers, flagged amber. No approval needed.
    10Everything elseRegisters.

    Consumption counts registered Yellow hours only. Anything pending is never counted against an allowance or a budget, and never appears in an on-screen total — which is why pending hours are shown separately, in amber, as In approval.

    Early warning

    Once you pick a project, a banner above the form tells you where you stand on that assignment: grey from 75 %, amber from 90 %, red at 100 % — "Further entries are accepted but routed to approval." You are never blocked; you are routed.

    Entry statuses

    StatusMeaning
    RegisteredCounted. Included in totals, budgets and exports.
    PendingWaiting on an approver. Excluded from every total. The row is tinted and shows its reason.
    CancelledDeclined. Stays visible to you with the approver's reason, and never contributes hours.
    LockedThe month was closed for payroll. Not editable; changing it needs a retroactive-edit request.

    Editing, deleting and correcting

    • ✎ loads an entry back into the form band. Saving a pending entry re-runs the engine; saving a registered entry as an approver leaves it registered.
    • 🗑 deletes it and cancels any pending approval attached. The toast offers Undo.
    • Click the Project status cell in a row to cycle it through the eight project statuses without opening the form.
    • You may always edit your own entries. Editing someone else's needs editOthersTime — Team Lead within their team, Project Manager within their projects, Operations Manager and CEO anywhere.

    Filtering and totals

    The month navigator moves one month at a time; Today returns to the current one. Below it, filter by employee, client, project, work order, task, entry type, project status and entry status. Clear resets every filter at once. Totals always describe the filtered view.

    Exporting

    ⬇ This view exports the filtered month. ⬇ Everything exports everything within your permission scope — which for an Employee, Senior Employee or External Contractor means their own records only. Pending entries are moved to a second worksheet so they can never be mistaken for reported time. See chapter 16.

    06

    Week Planner

    Intent, not record. Planned entries never count as reported hours — they exist so you can see the week before it happens and compare it afterwards.

    Planning a block

    1. Choose Day, Week or Month (a four-week grid), and move with ‹ › or This week.
    2. Click + plan in a day column.
    3. Set start and end — derived hours appear beneath — then hour type, client, project, work order, task and a description.
    4. Save. Drag the card to another day at any time.

    Suggestions from your tasks

    Assigned tasks that have a due date and are not yet Done or Cancelled appear as teal chips above the grid. Drag one onto a day and the planned entry is created pre-filled with that task's project, work order and title.

    Capacity, variance and copying

    • Planned, Actual and Variance chips compare the plan with registered time over the same span.
    • Capacity compares the plan against contracted hours (weekly hours, scaled to the visible span). Planning past capacity turns the chip amber and adds a banner.
    • Copy week duplicates every visible planned entry forward by seven days.
    • ⬇ Export produces a plan-against-actual worksheet with a variance column.

    Planning for someone else

    With planOthersWeek a Planning for selector appears. Team Leads see their team, Project Managers their projects' people, Operations Managers and the CEO everyone. Saving a change to someone else's plan notifies them.

    07

    Tasks

    Four views over the same task list: your own, a drag-and-drop board, a flat table and a per-project roll-up with consumption bars.

    What you can see

    • OPS, CEO and FIN see every task.
    • EXT sees only tasks explicitly assigned to them.
    • Everyone else sees tasks assigned to them, plus every task on a project they own or are assigned to.
    • VIEW has no Tasks tab.

    The four views

    My tasks — the board, filtered to you; the default view for Employees and contractors. Board — six columns: To do, In progress, Blocked, In review, Done, Cancelled. List — sorted by due date, with an inline status dropdown on every row. Project view — grouped by project, with hours consumed against allowance per task and in total.

    Creating and editing a task

    Click + New task, or click any card to edit. Title, project and description are all required — the description prompt asks what does done look like?

    FieldNotes
    Work orderRequired if the project is work-order tracked.
    StatusTo do · In progress · Blocked · In review · Done · Cancelled.
    PriorityLow · Normal · High · Critical. Shown as a coloured chip on the card.
    Hour allowanceBudgeted hours for the task. Drives the consumption bar on the card and the prompt when the task is closed.
    AssigneesSeveral people per task. A task with none sits in the project backlog. Finance and Viewer accounts cannot be assigned.
    DependenciesOther tasks on the same project that must reach Done first.
    Attachment or linkA URL or a file reference.
    CommentsAvailable once the task is saved.

    Blocking and unblocking

    A task with an unfinished dependency is forced to Blocked and cannot be dragged to another active column — the toast explains why. Completing the dependency releases every task waiting on it back to To do automatically.

    Closing a task

    Moving one of your own tasks to Done when you have reported less than half its allowance raises a prompt to report the remaining time first, or continue anyway.

    Overdue tasks

    A task past its due date and not Done or Cancelled is marked overdue on the card, shown in red in List view, and counted in the amber badge on the Tasks tab and in the manager cockpit.

    08

    Expenses

    Money or hours, attached to a client and project, with evidence where the expense type demands it.

    Recording an expense

    1. Set the Date and Time.
    2. Choose the Expense type. Types with a ceiling show it in the dropdown.
    3. Choose the Value kind: Money (with a currency) or Hours.
    4. Optionally attach it to a client, project and work order.
    5. Write a Description — required. Add notes if useful.
    6. Attach Evidence if the type requires it, then Add expense.
    Ceilings and evidence

    An expense at or under its type's auto-approval ceiling is approved on the spot. Above the ceiling it is saved as Submitted and routed to approval as above the ceiling for that type. Where the type requires evidence, the form will not submit without an attachment at all.

    Reimbursement status

    Four states — Submitted, Approved, Paid, Rejected. Anyone who may approve expenses changes it from the dropdown in the table; everyone else sees a coloured chip.

    Reporting for someone else

    With approveExpenses an Assignee selector appears on the form, scoped to the people you can see. The same people appear in the month filter.

    Editing, deleting and exporting

    ✎ loads the expense back into the form; 🗑 deletes it, with Undo. You may act on your own expenses always, and on other people's if you may approve expenses. ⬇ This view and ⬇ Everything export with evidence filenames listed in their own column.

    09

    Budgets and work orders

    What was agreed, what has been consumed, and when — at the current burn rate — it runs out.

    Who sees what

    Project Managers see budgets on their own projects. Operations Managers, the CEO and Finance see all of them; Finance may look but not change. Everyone else has no Budgets tab.

    Creating a budget

    1. Click + New budget.
    2. Pick the Client, then narrow to a Project and optionally a Work order. Leaving the project blank budgets all of that client's projects together.
    3. Choose a Pricing basis and fill the matching fields.
    4. Save budget.
    BasisWhat you enter
    MoneyCurrency, amount and hourly rate. Budgeted hours are derived — amount ÷ rate — and the field goes read-only.
    HoursBudgeted hours directly.
    Pages / Words / CustomBudgeted units, a unit name, how many are produced so far, and budgeted hours alongside.

    Reading a budget card

    FigureHow it is calculated
    ConsumedRegistered Yellow hours in the budget's scope. A work-order budget counts only that work order; a project budget counts the project.
    In approvalPending Yellow hours in the same scope. Not yet consumed — but coming.
    RemainingBudgeted minus consumed. Turns red when negative.
    Value consumedMoney basis only — consumed hours × rate.
    DeliverablesUnit bases only — produced against budgeted units.
    Projected exhaustionExtrapolated from the last 30 days' burn. Reads now if already exhausted, — if nothing has been burned.

    At 90 % the card shows an amber banner, visible to the project owner and Finance. At 100 % it turns red and every further Yellow entry on that project routes to approval.

    Change history

    Editing a budget's basis, amount, rate, hours, units or currency appends to its history. The History button opens a table of when, who, which field, and the before-and-after values.

    Work orders

    1. Switch to Work orders and click + New work order.
    2. Pick the project — WorkDesk proposes a code in the form WO-[client initials]-[6 digits]. You may override the digits, but the shape is enforced.
    3. Give it a title, a status (Active, On hold, Closed), a contract reference and dates.
    4. Enter Budgeted hours for this work order and a budget record is created or updated for you.
    Side effect worth knowing

    Creating the first work order on a project marks that project WO tracked. From then on, every Yellow entry on it must name a work order.

    10

    Approvals

    One queue for everything the engine could not decide by itself: time entries, allowance increases, unlisted list items, over-ceiling expenses, new accounts, role changes and retroactive edits.

    What lands in your queue

    • Requests routed to you directly.
    • Requests from anyone in your approval scope — your team for a Team Lead, your projects for a Project Manager.
    • OPS and CEO see everything: higher in the chain may always act.
    • Never your own. A request you raised is invisible to you as an approver, whatever your role.

    How a request is routed

    For most reasons, WorkDesk tries in order: your team's lead → your direct manager → a Project Manager → the Operations Manager → the CEO, skipping anyone who is inactive, cannot approve time, or is the requester.

    For Budget exceeded it skips the team entirely and goes Project Manager → Operations Manager → CEO, because the decision belongs to whoever owns the budget.

    The four actions

    ActionWhat happens
    ApproveThe entry registers, or the expense is approved, or the account is activated. The requester is notified.
    Edit & approveOpens the entry in a full Time Log form. Correct it, then Save & approve. Both the original submission and the corrected version are kept in the audit log. You may also set the person's allowance on that assignment while you are there.
    DeclineA written reason is mandatory. The entry becomes Cancelled, stays visible to the requester with your reason, and never contributes hours.
    Request informationSends a question. The request stays Pending and the exchange appears as a thread on the card.

    What approval quietly fixes

    • Unlisted item — the typed client or project becomes a real, verified master-list record, and the entry is re-pointed at it.
    • Not assigned — an assignment is created for that person on that project or work order, so the next entry does not re-trigger.
    • Allowance exceeded — you are asked to choose. Approve only registers this entry and leaves the allowance alone; Approve and raise allowance raises it by exactly the overage, so the next entry does not re-trigger either.
    • Account activation — confirm the role in the dropdown on the card before approving.

    Bulk approval

    Tick the checkbox on several cards and a Bulk approve button appears. Requests whose reason is Budget exceeded cannot be ticked and cannot be bulk-approved — they must be handled one at a time.

    Reviewing decisions

    Switch the Status selector to Approved or Declined to see history. You will see decisions you made, decisions routed to you, and — as OPS or CEO — everything. Each shows who decided, when, and any note.

    11

    Dashboard

    Registered time only, over a period you choose, with a manager cockpit on top for anyone who has people or budgets to watch.

    Scope

    With dashboardCompany you see everyone in your scope and get an Employee multi-select. Without it — Employee, Senior Employee — the Dashboard shows your own figures only. External contractors have no Dashboard at all.

    The manager cockpit

    Shown to Team Leads, Project Managers, Operations Managers, Finance and the CEO. Four tiles that turn colour when they need attention:

    • Outstanding approvals — requests waiting on you.
    • Projects over 90 % of budget — named in the subtitle.
    • People over capacity — planned beyond contracted hours this week.
    • Overdue tasks.

    The figures

    Total hours · Red · Yellow · Utilisation (Yellow as a share of total) · Sick days · Vacation days · Expenses per currency · Entries reported. Below them, hours over time as a stacked Red-and-Yellow area chart, hours by client as a ranked bar list, and two comparison bars: the Red/Yellow split and planned against actual with its variance.

    Filters and export

    Filter by employee, client, project, work order and task; Clear resets them. ⬇ Export writes the underlying entries — not the chart — so the numbers can be checked.

    12

    Library

    The house knowledge base — style decisions, terminology rulings, snippets, client conventions — searchable across titles, bodies and tags.

    Adding an item

    1. Click + New item.
    2. Give it a title — required — and a body.
    3. Add comma-separated tags and a link if there is one.
    4. Optionally attach it to a project and a task, and add an image.
    5. Tick Private to me if it is a personal note rather than house knowledge.
    6. Save.

    Who can edit what

    • Private items are visible only to their author.
    • You may edit or delete your own items. OPS and CEO may edit anyone's.
    • Finance can read the Library but not write to it. Contractors and Viewers have no Library tab.
    13

    My assignments and allowances

    The ◱ button in the top bar. Where your funded work lives, and where you ask for more hours before you run out.

    Requesting additional hours

    1. Click Request hours on the assignment that needs them.
    2. Read the four figures — allowance, consumed, remaining, in approval — then enter the additional hours.
    3. Write a justification. Both fields are required.
    4. Submit request.

    The request appears in the approval queue as an Allowance increase, showing the hours asked for and your justification. On approval, the allowance is raised automatically.

    If nothing opens

    The ◱ button does nothing when you hold no assignments at all. Ask an Operations Manager to assign you to a project — until then, every Yellow hour you report will route to approval as Not assigned.

    14

    Administration

    One modal behind the ⚙ button, with sections down its left side. Which sections you see depends on your role; the list is built for you each time you open it.

    People

    Everyone, with email, role, team, manager and status. Operations Managers and the CEO change roles from the dropdown here and can Deactivate or Reactivate an account. Everyone else sees the same list read-only. The Used by column counts the records each person is attached to.

    Pending activation

    New sign-ups wait here. For each one:

    1. Confirm or change the role they requested.
    2. Set their team.
    3. Give them an initial project and an allowance in hours.
    4. Activate — or Reject, which closes the account.

    Assignments & allowances

    The control that decides whose Yellow time registers quietly and whose goes to approval.

    Allowance valueEffect
    Blank (∞)Unlimited within the project budget.
    0Assigned but not yet funded — every entry goes to approval.
    A numberHours that person may report on that assignment before approval is required.

    Allowance is set per assignment, not per person: the same writer can hold 40 hours on one work order and 200 on another. Edit a number in place and it saves when you leave the field; 🗑 removes the assignment.

    Master lists — Clients, Projects, Work orders, Expense types

    Each is a table with an add band above it. Rename with ✎, remove with 🗑.

    Nothing in use can be deleted

    The Used by column counts the saved records that reference an item. If it is above zero, removal is refused. Deactivate or close it instead.

    Verification queue

    Clients and projects created by approving an unlisted item wait here until a manager clicks Confirm. It is the tidy-up step after a busy week of typed-in names.

    Company settings

    OPS and CEO only.

    • Daily soft limit (default 9 h) — entries above it register with an amber flag.
    • Daily hard limit (default 12 h) — entries above it route to approval.
    • Default weekly hours (default 42 h).

    Month close and payroll lock

    1. Pick the month and click Close month.
    2. If the month still holds pending requests, WorkDesk refuses and says how many. Clear the queue first.
    3. On close, every registered entry in that month becomes Locked and can no longer be edited.
    4. A closed month appears as a 🔒 chip with a reopen link, which unlocks its entries again.

    Audit log

    Append-only. Every sign-in, sign-out, role change, approval decision, budget edit, allowance change and deletion is recorded with actor, timestamp, entity and device. Visible to OPS, FIN and CEO; the most recent 400 entries are shown, 4,000 retained.

    15

    Backup and restore

    In a standalone build this is the only safety net there is. OPS and CEO only.

    The file format

    A valid backup is a JSON object with four top-level keys, one of which wraps everything else:

    KeyValue
    appMust be exactly "galil-workdesk". Anything else is refused.
    version7 for this build.
    exportedISO timestamp.
    dataThe object holding every entity: entries, projects, tasks, budgets, expenses, library, approvals, audit and the lists.

    The three operations

    OperationWhat it does
    Download backupWrites everything to galil-workdesk-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json.
    Restore everythingLoads everything, including entries, and replaces what is there. Choose the file, click Validate and preview, check the record counts, then Restore everything.
    Import lists onlyA different function. Loads dropdown definitions only — clients, projects, work orders, expense types and teams — and never touches entries. Existing items are matched by id, name or code and skipped.
    If storage fills up

    Browser storage is finite. If it fills, WorkDesk warns that the last change was not saved and asks you to download a backup and remove attachments. Take that message seriously — it means a write failed.

    Scope

    Export scope follows the permission matrix, not the screen. ⬇ Everything gives an Employee, Senior Employee or External Contractor their own records; a Team Lead their team; a Project Manager their projects; and Operations, Finance, the CEO and Viewers everything.

    Date columns carry real Excel dates formatted Sunday, 26/07/2026, so they sort and filter properly. Red and Yellow columns are accent-coloured to match the application.
