Signing in & accounts

WorkDesk is restricted to @galiltc.co.il email addresses. From the sign-in screen you can either sign in with a password, or use the simulated "Sign in with Microsoft" account picker.

Don't have an account yet? Use Create account on the sign-in screen. New accounts start in a pending activation state — someone with People-management rights (Operations Manager or CEO) needs to activate you before you can use the app. Self-selectable roles at sign-up are Employee, Senior Employee and Team Lead; anything above that (Project Manager and up) is assigned by an administrator afterwards.

Note — WorkDesk doesn't have a live server yet. Sign-in and the whole database are simulated in your browser (see Backup & restore). Real Microsoft Entra ID sign-in is on the roadmap.

Roles & what you can see

Every account has exactly one role, and the role decides which tabs you see and whose records you can view, edit or approve.

RoleCan generally do
EmployeeReport own time, plan own week, report own expenses, work assigned tasks, write to the Library.
Senior EmployeeAs Employee, plus create draft tasks within assigned projects and see the full task board.
Team LeadAs Senior Employee, plus approve time and expenses for their team and manage task assignment.
Project ManagerAs Team Lead, plus own projects, work orders, budgets and assignments.
Operations ManagerAs Project Manager, but across all clients — manages master lists, employees, roles and company settings.
FinanceRead-only across budgets, expenses, work orders and exports. Cannot approve time.
CEO / OwnerFull access, including role assignment, deletion, the audit log and company settings.
External ContractorReports time only against work orders they're explicitly assigned to. No budgets, Library or Dashboard.
ViewerRead-only dashboard access for a named stakeholder. No reporting rights.

The exact permission matrix (down to "own team only" vs. "own projects only" scopes) is visible to admins in Lists & data administration → Roles & permissions.

The top bar & tabs

The dark bar at the top of WorkDesk is always visible. From left to right:

  • The GALIL WorkDesk mark, and an Offline chip if your browser has lost its connection (your changes are still cached locally and will sync once you're back online).
  • ◱ My assignments and allowances — your personal list of budgeted work.
  • ⚙ Lists & data administration — only shown if your role can manage clients/projects, view the audit log, back up data, or assign tasks.
  • ? Help — opens this handbook in a new tab, from anywhere in the app.
  • Your avatar and name — opens My profile.

Below that is the tab bar. Which tabs you see depends on your role and permissions: Time Log, Week Planner, Tasks, Expenses, Budgets, Approvals, Dashboard and Library. A small badge on Approvals shows how many requests are waiting on you; a badge on Tasks shows how many of your tasks are overdue.

Time Log

This is where you report how a day was spent. Every entry has a date and a type — Work, Sick or Vacation. Work entries are timed (start/end, or a manual hour count) and split into two hour "colours":

  • Red hours — internal / company time, not billed to a client.
  • Yellow hours — client-billable time, and require a client, project and (usually) a work order.

You can attach a task, add a description and notes, and link a project status update to the same entry. Entries move through a lifecycle: registered → pending (if it needs approval) → approved/locked, or cancelled. Team Leads, Project Managers, Operations Managers and the CEO can view and edit time for the people in their scope; everyone else only sees their own.

The filter bar lets managers slice the log by employee, client, project, work order, task, entry type, project status and entry status — and export the filtered view straight to Excel (see Excel export).

Week Planner

A week-at-a-glance view for planning work ahead of time and comparing it against what actually got logged. Anyone can plan their own week; Team Leads and above can plan for the people in their scope.

Tasks

Tasks belong to a project (and optionally a work order), and carry a title, description, status, priority, start/due dates, an optional hour allowance, and one or more assignees. Senior Employees and above can create draft tasks on projects they're part of; Team Leads and above can assign tasks to other people. Updating your own task's status is available to everyone who can see it.

From the Tasks tab you can jump straight into reporting time against a task — WorkDesk opens the Time Log form with that task pre-selected.

Expenses

Report an expense the same way you report time: pick an expense type, a date, and either a money amount (with currency) or an hours value, then describe it and optionally link it to a client, project or work order. Attach receipts if you have them. Expenses go through the same Approvals workflow as time — submitted, then approved or declined by a Team Lead, Project Manager, Operations Manager or the CEO (scoped to their team/projects), with Finance able to view everything read-only.

My assignments & allowances

Open this from the ◱ button in the top bar. It lists every project/work order you're assigned to, your hour allowance on each, how much you've consumed, how much is sitting in pending approvals, and how much is left.

Approvals

Where Team Leads, Project/Operations Managers and the CEO review pending time and expense requests. Filter by type and status; approve, decline (with a note), ask the requester a question, or raise their hour allowance right from the request. The CEO and Operations Manager see everything; everyone else sees requests from people in their own scope, plus anything explicitly assigned to them.

Budgets & Work Orders

Where Project Managers and above set up and track the money and hours behind a project: budgets (by money, hours or unit basis, with a rate and currency), and the work orders that consume them. Finance has read-only access here; Project Managers are scoped to their own projects, while Operations Managers and the CEO see every project.

Dashboard

A rolled-up view of red (company) vs. yellow (client) hours, and — for anyone with company-wide dashboard rights — figures across the whole team, filterable by employee. Everyone with reporting rights can see their own dashboard; Team Leads and above (plus Finance and Viewer accounts) can see the company-wide figures within their scope.

Library

A searchable set of shared notes with tags — the place for "how we do X" knowledge that isn't formal enough for this handbook. Anyone who can report time can write a note; notes can be marked private to just the author. Search by keyword or filter by tag from the Library tab. External Contractors don't get Library access.

Backup & restore

WorkDesk doesn't have a shared server database yet — each browser keeps its own copy of the company's data in localStorage, so it's not shared between users or devices. Use Lists & data administration → Backup & restore to export the whole database as a JSON file, or to import one:

  • Restore everything — replaces your local copy entirely with the file's contents.
  • Import lists only — brings in just the master lists (clients, projects, work orders, expense types, teams) without touching people, time or approvals.
Tip — take a backup before trying anything you're unsure about, and use it to move a demo session between browsers or machines.

Excel export

Time Log, Planner and Budgets each offer an Excel export of the currently filtered view, built entirely in your browser — no server involved. What you can export is scoped the same way viewing is: your own records only, your team's, your projects', or everything, depending on your role.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

My changes disappeared after I switched computers.

That's expected for now — see Backup & restore. Export a backup on the old machine and import it on the new one.

I can't see a tab I expect to see.

Tabs and buttons are role-based (see Roles & what you can see). Ask an Operations Manager or the CEO to check your role and permissions under Lists & data administration → People.

The app says "Browser storage is full".

Take a backup, then remove old attachments (receipts, photos) to free up space — localStorage has a small size limit per browser.

Something looks wrong and I don't know why.

An Operations Manager or the CEO can check the Audit log (under Lists & data administration) to see exactly who changed what, and when.
